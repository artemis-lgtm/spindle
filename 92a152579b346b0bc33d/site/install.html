How it works

Two screws and a plug. The record stays yours.

Two ways to hang it. Cord showing takes two screws and about fifteen minutes. Cord hidden in the wall takes longer and two small holes. Times are our estimates from the design; we will update them once real units are hung.

Drawn from the current design files. Left to right: wall plate (1), hub (2), power lead (3), light strip on its aluminium and cork rings (4 to 6), backplate (7), felt pads (8), your record (9), clear washer, felt washer and cap (10 to 12).

How it holds on

A 4-inch steel plate screws to the wall with two screws, into a stud or into the wall anchors we include. You can turn the plate a little after the screws are in, so getting it level is easy.

The light pushes onto three pins on the plate and twists to lock. One small screw at the bottom stops it from ever twisting off by accident.

The record is held from the front by the dome cap, so changing a record never touches the mount.

Option A, the simple one

Cord showing. About 15 minutes, two screws.

The 6-foot cord plugs into a short lead tucked behind the light and runs down the wall to the outlet. Add a paintable cord cover from the hardware store if you want it neat, or let the cord be part of the look.

Renters: this is two screw holes, like a heavy picture frame.

Pick the spot

The centre of the record usually looks right 58 to 62 inches from the floor. Make sure the cord reaches an outlet.

Plate up

Hold the plate level, mark two holes, drive the screws, check level, tighten.

Twist the light on

Plug the cord into the light's lead. Push the light onto the plate, twist until it stops, snug the small screw.

Record, dome, plug

Record on the felt pads, dome through the centre hole by hand. Plug in and set the dimmer.

Option B

Cord hidden in the wall. No cord on show.

A thin low-voltage cable runs inside the wall from behind the light down to a small jack plate next to your outlet. The power adapter plugs in there, out in the open.

The one rule: only the thin low-voltage cable goes in the wall. The power adapter and its cord never go inside a wall, ceiling or floor.

This is Class 2 low-voltage wiring, and the cable must be rated for in-wall use (marked CL2 or better). Rules vary by town and by building, so check your local code. If you are not sure, or you are not comfortable cutting into a wall, ask an electrician.

You will cut a 1-inch hole behind the light and a small opening by the outlet, both in the same gap between wall studs, then feed the cable down. We estimate about 45 minutes with a stud finder and a drill.

The in-wall kit (rated cable, wall bracket, jack plate, pull string) will be offered as an add-on when you pay your balance. Its price is not set yet; you will see it before you pay.

Not for hardwiring. Spindle is a plug-in light. It is not a listed light fixture and should not be wired directly to house wiring or a wall switch.

Heat, honestly

Heat is the first thing a record owner asks about, so here is exactly what we know and what we do not.

How it is built. The LED strip is on the back of the backplate, facing the wall. It shines away from the record and cannot shine on it. The strip is stuck to an aluminium ring that spreads its warmth, and a cork ring and half an inch of wood sit between that and your record. The light uses about 4 watts.

What we calculated. In an 86 F room, the warmest spot on the record works out to about 92 F, roughly 6 F above the room. Record vinyl starts to lose its stiffness at roughly 120 F.

What we have not done yet. That number is a calculation. No unit has been built or measured. Before any Spindle ships, we run real units for two hours with temperature probes on the record. A unit passes only if the record stays within 9 F of the room. We will post the measured numbers on this page.

The real risk to any record is the room. Do not hang a record above a radiator or heater, or where direct sun hits it.

Safety basics

  • Indoors, on a dry wall. Not in a bathroom, over a stove, on a porch or anywhere damp.
  • Use only the power adapter that comes in the box.
  • Keep the adapter in the open at the outlet. Do not cover it and never put it or its cord inside a wall.
  • Not above a radiator, heater or fireplace, and not in direct sun.
  • If anything flickers, buzzes or smells warm, unplug it and email us.
