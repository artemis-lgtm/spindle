FAQ

Everything people ask before they reserve.

Heat, your record, wiring, renters, refunds. If yours is not here, email hello@example.com.

You pay the rest only when your sconce is built, tested and ready to ship, and you can take your $25 back at any point before then.

Will the light heat up or warp my record?

It is designed not to, and we will test it on real units before anything ships. The LED strip sits on the back of the backplate, facing the wall, so it shines away from your record. It is stuck to a thin aluminium ring that spreads its warmth, with a layer of cork and half an inch of wood between it and the record. The whole light uses about 4 watts, about what a small night light bulb uses.

Our heat calculation puts the record about 6 F warmer than the room (92 F in an 86 F room). Record vinyl starts to lose its stiffness at roughly 120 F. Our figure is a calculation, not a measurement, and a calculation can be wrong, which is why we test. Before the first unit ships we run the light for two hours with temperature probes on the record, and it only passes if the record stays within 9 F of the room. We will post the measured numbers here.

What can hurt a record is the room: do not hang any record above a radiator or heater, or where direct sun hits it.

What touches my record?

Felt and one clear washer. The record rests on soft felt pads on the backplate. A thin clear washer lies on the label so the cap never rubs the paper, and the cap has its own felt underneath. The cap's screw goes through the centre hole, the same hole a turntable uses, into a brass thread in the backplate. Nothing is glued, taped or drilled, so you can take the record down and play it.

Can I change the record?

Yes, that is the point. Unscrew the dome cap by hand, lift the record off, set the next one on, screw the cap back. No tools, and the light stays on the wall.

How does it get power? Do I need an electrician?

The standard setup needs no electrician. A 12-volt power adapter plugs into a normal outlet and a thin cord runs to the sconce. You can let the cord show, cover it with a paintable cord cover, or hide a low-voltage cable inside the wall with our optional kit.

Only the thin low-voltage cable may go inside a wall. The adapter and its cord always stay out in the open at the outlet. This is Class 2 low-voltage wiring. Rules for it vary, so check your local code or ask an electrician before you cut into a wall. Spindle is a plug-in light and is not made to be hardwired to house wiring.

Is it UL listed?

The Spindle itself is not UL listed. The plug-in power adapter we ship is a UL-listed Class 2 adapter, and it is the only part connected to house power. Everything we make (the wooden backplate, the mount and the cap) carries 12 volts or nothing at all. We check the adapter's UL file number before we ship any unit. If your building or landlord requires the whole light fixture to be listed, this is not the right product for that wall yet. For the same reason we do not ship to the City of Los Angeles or North Carolina for now.

I rent. Can I still hang it?

Yes. The standard setup is two screws in the wall, like a heavy picture frame, and a cord down to the outlet. Hiding the cable inside the wall means cutting two small holes, which you would need to patch when you leave, so check your lease first.

How bright is it? Can I dim it?

It is a mood light, not a reading light. The light is warm white, about the colour of an old filament bulb, and it glows on the wall around the record. A dimmer on the cord takes it from full glow down to faint. There is no colour changing and no remote, on purpose.

Which records work?

Any standard 12-inch LP, including coloured vinyl and picture discs. A badly warped record will not sit flat. 7-inch singles do not fit the Spindle 12; a 7-inch version is planned for later.

What record comes with the $129 Spindle 12?

A real, used LP from the decade you choose (60s, 70s or 80s). We pick the title, clean it and check it for looks. These are old records chosen for display, so expect some wear, and we do not promise they play well. If you want a specific album, the $179 Named option lets you choose from a short menu, which we email you before you pay your balance. We never show cover art or logos and we are not affiliated with any artist or label.
