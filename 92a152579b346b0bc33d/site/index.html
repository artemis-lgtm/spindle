The questions everyone asks

Heat, your record, wiring, refunds.

Is this a finished product I can buy today?

Not yet, and we would rather say so. The design and engineering are done. The first units have not been built or tested yet. Today you can place a refundable $25 hold. You pay the rest only when your sconce is built, tested and ready to ship, and you can take your $25 back at any point before then.

Will the light heat up or warp my record?

It is designed not to, and we will test it on real units before anything ships. The LED strip sits on the back of the backplate, facing the wall, so it shines away from your record. It is stuck to a thin aluminium ring that spreads its warmth, with a layer of cork and half an inch of wood between it and the record. The whole light uses about 4 watts, about what a small night light bulb uses.

Our heat calculation puts the record about 6 F warmer than the room (92 F in an 86 F room). Record vinyl starts to lose its stiffness at roughly 120 F. Our figure is a calculation, not a measurement, and a calculation can be wrong, which is why we test. Before the first unit ships we run the light for two hours with temperature probes on the record, and it only passes if the record stays within 9 F of the room. We will post the measured numbers here.

What can hurt a record is the room: do not hang any record above a radiator or heater, or where direct sun hits it.

What touches my record?

Felt and one clear washer. The record rests on soft felt pads on the backplate. A thin clear washer lies on the label so the cap never rubs the paper, and the cap has its own felt underneath. The cap's screw goes through the centre hole, the same hole a turntable uses, into a brass thread in the backplate. Nothing is glued, taped or drilled, so you can take the record down and play it.

How does it get power? Do I need an electrician?

The standard setup needs no electrician. A 12-volt power adapter plugs into a normal outlet and a thin cord runs to the sconce. You can let the cord show, cover it with a paintable cord cover, or hide a low-voltage cable inside the wall with our optional kit.

Only the thin low-voltage cable may go inside a wall. The adapter and its cord always stay out in the open at the outlet. This is Class 2 low-voltage wiring. Rules for it vary, so check your local code or ask an electrician before you cut into a wall. Spindle is a plug-in light and is not made to be hardwired to house wiring.

Is the $25 really refundable? When do I pay the rest?

Yes. Any time before your sconce ships, for any reason, email us and the $25 goes back to your card within 5 business days. When your sconce is ready we email you a link to pay the rest (the price minus your $25). Nothing is charged automatically.
