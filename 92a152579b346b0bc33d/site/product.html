Questions before you reserve

Will the light heat up or warp my record?

No. The LED ring sits on the back of the backplate, facing the wall, where it cannot see the record at all. It is mounted on a thin aluminium ring that spreads its warmth, with a layer of cork and half an inch of wood between it and the record. The whole ring draws about 4 watts, and never more than 6, spread over a 28-inch circle. Vinyl starts to soften around 130 to 140 F; our heat calculation puts the record about 6 F above the room. The only way this record gets hot is if you hang it over a radiator or in direct sun, which the hang sheet tells you not to do. Our acceptance test for every batch is record face under 95 F in a 75 F room after a two-hour soak, and we publish the numbers once measured.

Is my record safe? What touches it?

Felt and one clear washer. The record rests on six felt pads and a felt ring on the backplate; a thin clear polyester washer lies on the label so the cap never turns against the paper, and the cap's own felt sits on that. The cap carries its own threaded stud, which passes through the spindle hole into a brass insert in the backplate; the smooth collar under the cap is a hair smaller than the hole, so nothing is forced and the record centres itself. You could take the record off tomorrow and play it. Three pads near the edge, three part-way in and the ring behind the label hold it flat, which is more support than a record gets in a sleeve on a shelf.
