About

Built in Fort Lauderdale by people who could not find this anywhere.

The idea was simple and the market had nothing: a wall light built around a real record that looked like a lighting fixture, not a craft project. What existed was either a resin miniature with three AAA batteries, a glued stack of LPs on a wooden arm, or an acrylic "now playing" shelf. Nobody was treating the record as the object and the light as the frame.

So we designed one the way a lighting company would. Low-voltage Class 2 power from a listed adapter. A steel plate that levels and bolts to any box. A dotless 2700K ring that throws its light at the wall and never at the vinyl. And one piece of real metal at the centre, because the only thing you touch should feel like it cost something.

The rule we will not break: nothing is ever glued, drilled or stuck to a record. Your pressing goes on the wall and comes off the wall exactly as it went on.

The first 20 went to friends and the people who saw the prototype on a wall and asked where to get one. Batch two is the first one open to everyone, capped at 100 so we can hand-build every unit and keep the promise on the ship date.
