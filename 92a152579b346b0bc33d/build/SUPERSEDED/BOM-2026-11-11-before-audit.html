4b. Thermal limit on the halo strip (pessimist review, 2026-11-10)

Line 8 (LED strip) is capped at 6 W per 12 in sconce (about 7 W/m). Buy the low-power 2835 60/m (1.46 W/ft) or a COB of no more than 8 W/m; do not buy the 10-12 W/m COB class. Reason and acceptance test: cad/README.md "Open questions" 7 and cad/label-light/REVIEW-pessimist.md.

Update 2026-11-11 (heat-safe layout, cad/THERMAL.md): on the 12 in sconce the strip is now on the back of the backplate on an aluminium ring (line 8a) over a cork ring (line 8b). Calculated record temperature with a 4 W strip in a 30 C room: 33.1 C. Stacked worst case (wrong 6 W strip, still air, 35 C room): 45.2 C. Vinyl softens at 55 to 60 C. These are calculations; nothing has been measured. The 7 in unit and the aluminium-backplate variants are not on this layout.
