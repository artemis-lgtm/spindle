# Record Sconces: bill of materials (build/BOM.md)

Prepared 2026-11-10 (overnight build). Nothing ordered, nobody contacted. Prices are per unit at the quantity we would buy for 1 / 20 / 100 / 500 units. Machine-readable copy: `BOM.csv`; the economics that use these lines: `cost-model.csv` (generated by `cost-model.py`, which is the single place the numbers live).

Fetch status codes used in every line:
- V = price confirmed on the vendor page by curl during this build (2026-11-09 night to 11-10).
- P = confirmed on the page 11/9 by the engineering or mounting research; tonight the host rate-limited (HTTP 429) or bot-blocked (403) our IP because several agents were hitting it at once. Re-check before ordering.
- S = search-snippet price only; page did not render for a scraper.
- E = estimate. Not a quote. Named as such.

Design decisions are taken from BRIEF.md, engineering-report.md, mounting-report.md and design-report.md and are not re-argued here. Where the sibling agents (dome-cap, inwall-parts) sent final picks tonight, those are used verbatim and credited.
