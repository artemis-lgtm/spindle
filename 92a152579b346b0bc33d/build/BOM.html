Record Sconces: bill of materials (build/BOM.md)

Prepared 2026-11-10 (overnight build). Nothing ordered, nobody contacted. Prices are per unit at the quantity we would buy for 1 / 20 / 100 / 500 units. Machine-readable copy: BOM.csv; the economics that use these lines: cost-model.csv (generated by cost-model.py, which is the single place the numbers live).

Fetch status codes used in every line: - V = price confirmed on the vendor page by curl during this build (2026-11-09 night to 11-10). - P = confirmed on the page 11/9 by the engineering or mounting research; tonight the host rate-limited (HTTP 429) or bot-blocked (403) our IP because several agents were hitting it at once. Re-check before ordering. - S = search-snippet price only; page did not render for a scraper. - E = estimate. Not a quote. Named as such.

Design decisions are taken from BRIEF.md, engineering-report.md, mounting-report.md and design-report.md and are not re-argued here. Where the sibling agents (dome-cap, inwall-parts) sent final picks tonight, those are used verbatim and credited.

6. Lead times (vendor published or typical; none are quotes)

  • Albany County Fasteners, Durofelt, Adafruit, Ecolocity, Outwater, avoutlet, Zack: stock, ship 1 to 2 business days.
  • Walmart / Amazon shells, adapters, COB: 2 to 5 days.
  • SendCutSend plate: 3 to 7 business days laser, plus powder coat.
  • PETG hubs: in-house overnight per 4 to 6 parts on one printer; a print service 5 to 10 days.
  • AliExpress shells at 100+: 2 to 4 weeks.
  • Custom turned chrome dome (post-deposit): 3 to 6 weeks.
  • CNC backplates (local shop): 1 to 2 weeks for 100.
