If the local CNC shop is slow, a DIY backplate is possible from the Home Depot panel (O2) with a router circle jig, a drill and a 3/8 in straight bit for the rear channel, but every pocket depth on the drawing matters, so a shop is the better route for a sample that gets heat tested.

4. Things to know before ordering

  • Austin's printer. Line C4 assumes the Bambu Lab P2S noted in memory/facts/active-products.md is still on hand. If not, Craftcloud takes the STL without an account, but its upload stalled in my browser and I have no price.
  • Strip layout. The ring is seven straight 100 mm pieces of strip with six short 22 AWG jumpers, not one bent piece. A flat strip cannot curve sideways in its own plane.
  • First thing after building: thermocouple test T1 on the record back at r 114 mm. Nothing has been measured yet.
