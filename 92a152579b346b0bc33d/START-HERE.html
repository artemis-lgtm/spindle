Where the LEDs go

Today the shop files put the light strip on the rim of the black disc. The record's edge hangs out past that rim, so the light sits an eighth of an inch from bare vinyl. A heat study on 11 Nov says the record stays around 95°F in a warm room, but the worst case gets too close to the 130°F where vinyl softens.

My pick: move the strip to the back of the disc, facing the wall, on a thin metal ring with a cork ring behind it. The record drops to about 91°F and the light can no longer see the vinyl. It is also how your approved photos were lit.

The shop files are not changed yet. Say yes and the CAD, parts list and shop packets all get updated together. The heat numbers.

Where the pieces disagree

Found cross-checking all eight folders this morning.

  • Kit price. Brief, site and cost model say $89; build and engineering both say it is too thin. Decision 2 above.
  • LED placement. Renders put the ring on the back of the plate facing the wall; CAD, parts list and the site FAQ describe it on the rim edge. Decision 3 above, then CAD and the FAQ line get updated.
  • Parts cost per 12 inch unit at 100. Engineering said $27, build says $38, sourcing says $41 on a mixed US and overseas path or $57 all-US. The gap is the custom chrome dome and the steel plate added after engineering. The cost model uses $38; treat $41 as the honest planning number until real quotes come back.
  • Prototype budget. Build's $400 is three hand-built units with stand-in parts this week; sourcing's $1,300 to $1,700 is eight units with the real parts in about three weeks. Both are needed, in that order.
  • Site footer. Said "Side A, always." after the name changed to Spindle. Fixed this morning to "Change the record. Change the room."
  • "Every US jurisdiction". The site FAQ says in-wall low voltage cable is fine in every US jurisdiction. Mostly true, slightly overstated. I'd soften it to "most US homes"; your call since it is launch copy.
  • Sourcing placeholders. Sourcing made rough stand-in shapes for quote tools before the CAD existed. The real files in cad/ replace them; quote requests should attach the cad quote packets.
  • 7 inch cap. Not quoted by anyone yet; it is carried at the 12 inch dome cost.
