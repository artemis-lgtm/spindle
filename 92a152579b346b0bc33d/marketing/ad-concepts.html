Spindle: ad concepts (14)

Prepared 2026-11-09. Nothing produced or posted yet. Each concept lists: the hook in the first 2 seconds, script and shot list, on-screen text, primary text, headline, CTA, which render or UGC shot it needs, and the proven ad or viral format it is modeled on with a link. Reference links are in the "Proven references" section at the end (verified by curl or WebFetch on 11/9 where marked; the Meta Ad Library itself returns 403 to curl and needs a logged-in browser, so Ad Library links are given as the brand search URL and should be opened in Chrome).

Compliance on every concept: no band, artist or album names or logos; plain-label records only in shots; "sold out" only as "First batch of 20 sold out"; no ship date unless set; no stock counters; claims match the landing page. Formats: 9:16 for Reels/Stories/TikTok, 1:1 and 4:5 for feed. Sound-off first: every video must work muted with text.

Renders available now (render/): sconce_hero_chrome.png, sconce_hero_brass.png, sconce_hero_black.png (straight-on hero on a warm wall, halo lit), sconce_exploded_chrome.png (wall, backplate with LED rim, standoff, record, felt washer, chrome dome), sconce_v2_cutaway.png (in-wall wiring), sconce_v2_rear.png (steel plate + twist-lock hub). renders-final/ is adding the set and detail shots overnight. UGC shots need one physical unit and a phone.

1. The switch-on

Hook (0-2s): pitch-black frame, a click, the wall blooms amber around a black disc. Script / shots: (0-2s) black, click, halo fades up. (2-6s) slow push-in on the lit record, chrome dome catching the room. (6-10s) hand turns the inline dimmer, halo pulls in to deep amber. (10-13s) end card. On-screen text: "Your record, lit." then "First batch of 20 sold out" then "Batch two: $25 refundable deposit". Primary text: Flip the switch. The wall goes amber, the grooves catch the light, the chrome dome reflects the room. That's it. That's the product. First batch of 20 sold out; batch two deposits are open, $25 and refundable. Headline: Your record, lit. CTA: Learn more. Needs: UGC clip, dark room, phone on a tripod, exposure locked for the wall glow. Fallback: animate sconce_hero_chrome.png from black with a brightness ramp. Modeled on: the "lights off, lights on" reveal that every lighting DTC (Govee, Nanoleaf, Poplight) runs as its evergreen ad; see references R1, R2.

2. The swap (ten seconds)

Hook: hands unscrew a chrome dome off a record on a lit wall. Script / shots: (0-2s) tight on hands, dome unthreading, real audio. (2-5s) record lifts off, halo still on, backplate visible for a beat. (5-8s) new record (different label colour) drops onto the felt pads, dome threads on. (8-12s) pull back to the new record lit. (12-15s) end card. On-screen text: "Change the record" / "change the room" / "Nothing glued. 10 seconds." Primary text: Change the record, change the room. The Spindle is a halo sconce that holds any 12" LP on a felt-padded plate with a polished chrome cap. Ten seconds to swap. Warm, dimmable, nothing touches your record but felt. Made to order in Fort Lauderdale. Headline: Swap the record in ten seconds CTA: Learn more. Needs: UGC, two plain-label records, macro-capable phone. The exploded render (sconce_exploded_chrome.png) as a 1-second insert at 5s to show the stack. Modeled on: "satisfying" product-mechanism clips (Art Vinyl flip-frame swap videos, magnetic-mount demos); the format where the mechanism is the whole ad. References R3, R9.

3. "Does it damage the record?" (objection killer)

Hook: text "Does it damage the record?" over a macro of a dome tightening on a label. Script / shots: (0-2s) question on screen. (2-5s) macro: felt washer under the dome. (5-8s) macro: three felt pads on the plate, record set down on them. (8-11s) hand on the rim of the backplate: "the light is out here, 12 volts, cool". (11-14s) record lifted off clean, label untouched. End card. On-screen text: "Felt washer" / "Felt pads" / "12V ring on the rim, nothing warm" / "Comes off exactly as it went on". Primary text: People ask if it damages the record. Felt pads, felt washer, finger-tight chrome dome, cool 12V light on the rim. Nothing warm, nothing sticky, nothing touching the grooves. The record comes off exactly as it went on. Headline: Nothing glued to your record. Ever. CTA: Learn more. Needs: UGC macro shots. Engineering flex insert: sconce_exploded_chrome.png. Modeled on: FAQ / "reply to comment" style ads that lead with the top objection (common in Tushy, Ruggable, Hexclad creative where the first line is the doubt). References R6, R7.

4. Renter install in 60 seconds

Hook: "Designer sconce. Renter install. 15 minutes." over a drill going into a wall. Script / shots: time-lapse: (0-3s) plate held to wall with a level. (3-8s) two screws, plate on. (8-14s) hub twists on with a click, set screw. (14-20s) braided cord down to the outlet in a paintable channel. (20-26s) record on, dome on. (26-30s) lights off, switch on, halo. On-screen text: step labels "1 plate" "2 twist" "3 plug" "4 record" and "comes off when you move". Primary text: Renters: a designer wall light you can actually install. Steel plate, two screws, twist-lock, plug in. Fifteen minutes. The braided cord runs down the wall or, if you own the place, the low-voltage cable goes inside it. Takes your records. Comes with you when you move. Headline: Plug-in. No electrician. CTA: Learn more. Needs: UGC time-lapse; sconce_v2_rear.png as a 1-second insert at the twist-lock step. Modeled on: Poplight's install videos (the renter-friendly sconce that went viral on the "no electrician" promise) and Wooj's two-screw mounting clips. References R1, R4.

5. Hide the cord in the wall (legally)

Hook: "There is no cord." Camera pans down a bare wall under a lit record. Script / shots: (0-3s) lit record, pan down, nothing on the wall. (3-8s) cut to the cutaway render (sconce_v2_cutaway.png) showing the CL2 cable dropping inside the wall to a plate by the outlet. (8-14s) UGC: hole saw, cable fished, keystone plate clicked in. (14-18s) back to the lit wall, text. On-screen text: "Only the 12V side goes in the wall" / "No electrician" / "45 minutes". Primary text: Hide the cord in the wall, legally. The Spindle runs 12 volts only, so the cable can go inside the wall to a small plate by the outlet, no electrician. The adapter stays at the outlet. Or run the braided cord down the wall. Your choice at install. Headline: In-wall wiring, no visible cord CTA: Learn more. Needs: sconce_v2_cutaway.png plus a UGC install sequence. Modeled on: TV-mount "hide your cables" content (PowerBridge-style before/after) which is a reliably high-save format in #homedecor. Reference R10.

Launch order and test plan

Week 1 (3 ads, $15/day, one Purchase ad set): 1 (switch-on), 2 (swap), 14 (the 20) as a still if no bench footage. Week 2: replace the lowest CTR with 3 (objection) and 4 (renter). Week 3 (holiday): add 7 and 8 (gift) in their own ad set if week-one CPD was under $45. Week 4: 10 (Set) for the home-bar audience, 6 (price anchor) as the static. Keep 5, 9, 11, 12, 13 as organic first; promote whichever clears 10k organic views.

Prune rule (Ghost Audio precedent): after 1,000 impressions, pause anything under 0.5% CTR or over 2x the median cost per result. Six live ads maximum.
