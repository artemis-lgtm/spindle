# Spindle: ad copy bank

Prepared 2026-11-09. Compliance rules applied to every line: no band, artist, album or label names; no logos; "sold out" appears only as "first batch of 20 sold out" (true per Austin 11/9); no ship date unless Austin has set one; no "UL listed" about the finished fixture (only the adapter is listed); no stock counts or countdowns not wired to Stripe; no "limited edition"; claims match the landing page (Meta reviews both, https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/). Headlines are kept under 40 characters where possible (Meta truncates around 40 on mobile; Meta's Ads Guide recommends 27 characters for the headline field; facebook.com/business/ads-guide needs a logged-in browser, it returned 400 to curl on 11/9). Primary texts keep the hook in the first 125 characters, which is what shows before "See more".

Brand voice (brand/brand-guide.html): plain, specific, a little dry; no exclamation marks; never "elevate", "vibe", "aesthetic", "curated". Secondary lines from the guide: "Your record. Our light." (Kit), "The sconce that takes requests." (headline test B), "Twelve inches of what you love, lit at 2700 K." (long-form subhead).

Prices used: Kit $89, Sconce with record $129, Set from $249, deposit $25 refundable.

## CTA buttons

## Lines that were cut and why
- "Sold out" alone: untrue without "first batch of 20".
- "UL listed sconce": only the adapter and components are listed.
- "Ships before Christmas": no ship date is set.
- Any "limited edition", "only X left", "ends tonight" without a Stripe-wired number and a real date.
- "Framed like the [artist] record in your dad's basement": artist name.
- "Rumours on the wall": album name in copy.
- "The vinyl sconce": Diesel Living with Lodes already sells a "Vinyl" wall light; avoid as a product name.
