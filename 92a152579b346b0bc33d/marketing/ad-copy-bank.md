# Spindle: ad copy bank (v2)

Rewritten 2026-11-11 after the audit (CHANGELOG.md). v1 had 50 headlines and 30 primary texts; a third of them stated things that are not true yet or not provable (a built first batch, a cool-running light, a ten-second swap, "made in Fort Lauderdale", a Set that cannot be sold). This bank is smaller and every line is tagged with the gate at which it becomes usable (gates are in strategy.md section 3) and the proof it leans on (claims register, strategy.md section 2).

Rules for every line: no band, artist, album or label names; no other brand's name; no "UL" in a headline or on screen; no ship date, countdown or stock number that is not real; no "made in"; no adjective about heat ("cool", "safe", "nothing warm"), only the measured number once it exists; "[N]" placeholders are filled from footage, never guessed. Headlines stay under about 40 characters. The first sentence of a primary text must stand alone.

Voice (brand/brand-guide.html): plain, specific, a little dry. No exclamation marks. Never "elevate", "vibe", "aesthetic", "curated".

Prices: none in any line until Austin re-sets them from a real cap quote. At today's costs the $89 Kit loses $19.90 an order and the $129 Sconce clears $12.77 (strategy.md section 4). [PRICE] and [KIT PRICE] are placeholders. Finish: chrome only; brass and black caps have no real part yet. Deposit $25, refundable. The Set and the named-title tier have no copy here because neither can be sold yet.

## Lines cut in this audit, and why

- "We built 20. They sold." and "We sold the first 20 before the website existed. Now the website exists." Nothing has been built. The second line returns in a true form as video concept 13.
- "First batch of 20 sold out" before Gate 3. Twenty reported pre-sales with no ledger and no units is not a sold-out batch. "The first 20 are spoken for" is the honest version from Gate 2.
- "Your record is safer on this wall than in the crate." A light and a clamp cannot be safer than a sleeve. Indefensible.
- "Cool 12 volt ring", "nothing warm", "no heat near the vinyl", "cannot warp". Unmeasured. The thermal model's stacked worst case is 45.2 C and record compound starts to soften at 48 to 50 C (../audits/AUDIT-product.md). Replaced by the measured-number line.
- "Nothing touches your record but felt." Not true to the current design: a clear PET washer sits on the label under the cap (../cad/FIXES-fable52.md).
- "Three felt pads." There are six, plus a backing ring (../build/BOM.md lines 6 and 6a). Copy now says "felt pads" with no count.
- "Swap in ten seconds", "one hand", "15-minute install", "45 minutes in-wall". Unmeasured. Replaced with [N] from footage.
- "Made in Fort Lauderdale", "made to order in Fort Lauderdale", "built to order in Fort Lauderdale". Replaced with "assembled" and, in body copy, "from US and imported parts" (strategy.md section 2 has the FTC source).
- "Dotless" and "CRI 90+" as fixed claims. ../build/BOM.md allows either a COB strip or a 2835 60-per-metre strip for heat reasons, and a 2835 strip is not dotless. Use these words only after the strip is bought and its datasheet is on file.
- "UL-listed 12V adapter" in headlines and on-screen text. Next to the product name it reads as "the light is UL listed". It stays on the product page with "the fixture itself is not UL listed".
- "Hide the cord in the wall (legally)" and "no electrician" for in-wall. Replaced by the softer wording.
- "$755 look. $129 price.", "The Tom Dixon look for the price of a nice dinner", "The halo sconce, minus the designer markup". A named designer in an ad, and a comparison between a real $755 product and a product that has not been built.
- "Light the record, not a sign" as a claim that nobody else lights the record. Vylumi sells a back-lit record display (https://vylumi.com/). The line can describe us; it cannot imply we are the only one.
- "Chrome, brass or black" and every brass, black or lit-cap line. No real part exists for those finishes and the lit cap's contact has never been built.
- Every price. Held until a real cap quote re-sets them.
- All Set lines ("Three records, three halos, from $249", "Cheaper than two sconces"). The 7 in is not on the heat-safe layout; the Set is off sale.
- "Deposit holders pick finish and first ship slot" before a ship week exists.
- From v1's own cut list, still cut: "sold out" alone, "UL listed sconce", "ships before Christmas", "limited edition", "only X left", any artist or album reference, "the vinyl sconce".
