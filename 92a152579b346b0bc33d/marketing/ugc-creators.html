Program design

Wave 1 (11/12): gift 10 Kits ($89 retail, about $45 landed each, no cash) to the ten best-fit vinyl and listening-room creators under 120K. Ask for nothing but honesty; most will post because the product films itself (switch-on, swap). Wave 2 (11/19): 10 more, weighted to apartment decor and home bar. From month two, paid deals with 3 to 5 mid-tier creators whose gifted video performed, plus whitelisting (Partnership Ads) so their video runs from our ad account.

Rules: - Every creator post is an endorsement under the FTC Endorsement Guides; gifted product counts as a material connection, so the creator must disclose (#ad or #gifted, "Spindle sent me this") in the caption and on screen (16 CFR Part 255, Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements, https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-255, verified 200 on 11/9; ftc.gov itself returned 404 to curl that night). We put the disclosure line in the brief. - No scripts. A one-page brief: what it is, what not to say (no band names or logos on camera, no ship dates, no "sold out" beyond "first batch of 20 sold out"), and three shots we would love (switch-on in the dark, the ten-second swap, where they hung it). Plain-label records: we include two plain-label records in every creator box so they never have to show a logo to show the product. - Usage rights: gift brief asks for permission to reshare organically; paid deals buy 90 days of whitelisting and paid usage, in writing. - Tracking: each creator gets a personal link (utm_source=creator, utm_content=handle) and the deposit page shows their handle in the referral field. No discount codes (no month-one discounting). - Approach rule: gift-only under about 30K; gift first then paid from 30K to 150K; paid above 150K.
