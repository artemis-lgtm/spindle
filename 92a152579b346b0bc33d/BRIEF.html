The product (decided)

  • Halo-backlit wall sconce built around a real 12" vinyl LP (7" single variant). Warm 2700K COB LED ring on the rim of a ~10.5" backplate throws a halo on the wall. 12V Class 2 from a UL-listed adapter. Nothing 120V inside.
  • Customer's own record screws on with a mirror-CHROME DOME cap (brass and gloss black variants) on a 1/4-20 post through the spindle hole into a brass insert. Felt washer. Nothing glued to customer records.
  • Wall: steel mounting plate (fits box or anchors), hub twist-locks on, set screw. Wiring BEHIND THE WALL is the default (CL2 cable inside the wall down to an outlet-height jack plate); surface cord + raceway is option B.
  • SKUs: Kit (no record, customer's own) ~$89; Sconce with a vintage record $129; named title $179; 3-piece Set (12" + two 7") ~$249 kit / $329 with records. 20 units already sold (first batch sold out, real).
  • Legal: NO band names, logos, or album art in our marketing or renders. Use plain/fictional labels.
  • Brand name not final. Candidates: "Spindle" product, brand "Deadwax" or "Side A". Check before using.
