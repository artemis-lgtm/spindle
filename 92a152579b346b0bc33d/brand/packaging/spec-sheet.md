## 2. Insert tray

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Footprint | 12.75 x 12.75 in (0.125 in clearance per side in a 13 x 13 interior). |
| Layers | Two die-cut layers: Layer 1 top 1.25 in, Layer 2 lower 1.5 in, record in its poly-lined sleeve flat under Layer 2 on the box floor (0.2 in). Total 2.95 in in a 3.0 in interior. |
| Layer 1 cutouts | Centre: Ø2.0 in through-pocket for the dome cap, face up, sitting exactly on the spindle axis of the record beneath. Lower left: cord and adapter pocket (ring segment). Upper right: wall plate and hardware pocket 2.9 x 1.5 in. Upper left: card slot 2.9 x 0.35 in. |
| Layer 2 cutouts | Ø10.75 in pocket for the backplate and LED ring, with a Ø4.2 in relief in the centre for the hub. |
| Material | First 100 units: black polyethylene foam (EPE), 1.7 pcf, die-cut, from a US foam converter (Foam Factory, UFP, or a local Uline-stocked converter; ask Packlane for their insert partner). At 500 plus: black moulded pulp tray from the same tooling drawing. Moulded pulp is the Gantri and Teenage Engineering move; foam is the right call before tooling is justified (estimate: pulp tooling $2,500 to $6,000). |
| Artwork | brand/packaging/insert-tray.svg: plan views of both layers and a centre section with dimensions. |
| Why this layout | The dome is the single piece of jewellery on the product, so it is the first thing seen. The record comes last, under everything, protected by two layers of foam: a record arriving warped or cracked is the one unboxing failure that cannot be fixed with an apology. |

## 6. Unboxing order (what the packer does)
1. Record in poly-lined inner sleeve, flat on the box floor, label up.
2. Layer 2 foam over it; backplate with the LED ring face down into the Ø10.75 pocket, hub nested in the relief.
3. Layer 1 foam; wall plate, screws, anchors, felt washer and 45 adapter in the hardware pocket; adapter and cord coiled in the ring pocket; insert card and warranty card in the slot.
4. Dome cap last, face up, in the centre pocket.
5. Close, seal the tuck with the round sticker, apply the finish label to the front-wall target.
