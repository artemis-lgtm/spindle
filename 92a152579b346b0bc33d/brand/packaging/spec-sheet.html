Packaging spec sheet: Spindle 12 mailer, tray, cards, stickers

Prepared 2026-11-09 for a Packlane or Arka quote request. Nothing ordered. Prices below are the vendors' own published numbers or marked (estimate).

1. Mailer box

Item Spec
Style Roll-end tuck-top (RETT) mailer with dust flaps. Packlane lists it as "Roll end front tuck (with dust flaps)" on the mailer-box product page (https://packlane.com/products/mailer-box).
Interior size 13 x 13 x 3 in (L x W x D). Custom size; Packlane's size picker accepts custom L/W/D alongside its 14 standard sizes, none of which is 13 x 13 x 3 (closest stock: 12 x 10 x 4 and 13 x 10 x 5).
Board Kraft B-flute corrugated (Packlane material options: White, White B-flute, Dreamcoat, Dreamcoat B-flute, Kraft, Kraft B-flute). Kraft is the brand choice: the ink sits on fibre, the black reads as vinyl, and kraft hides scuffs from carriers.
Print, outside Digital CMYK, 2 inks in practice: K (rich black built as C60 M50 Y50 K100 is NOT wanted on kraft; use K only) plus Ember (C32 M62 Y100 K20 approx; vendor to match to #B9661C on a kraft drawdown). Packlane print finishes available: HDPrint matte, HDPrint satin, Gloss, Matte. Specify HDPrint matte.
Print, inside Yes. Lid interior flood Soot (#2B2622, approx C70 M66 Y66 K78) with Halo radial gradient (#F0A43B to #B9661C to Soot) and Label-colour type. Bottom interior: hairline K frame and one line of mono type. Side walls and flaps unprinted. Packlane prints inside on every box style; select "Printed sides: Both Sides" (https://packlane.com/products/mailer-box).
Artwork files brand/packaging/mailer-13x13x3-outside.svg and mailer-13x13x3-inside.svg. Vector, all text outlined, 72 pt per inch, 0.125 in bleed drawn, 0.25 in safe zone drawn, cut lines magenta, creases cyan dashed, legend on sheet. Export to PDF/X-4 from Illustrator or Inkscape before upload. Label uploads "Box Exterior" and "Box Interior" as Packlane asks.
Dieline note Our dieline is a placement dieline built from the panel maths (W 13 + 4 walls of 3 + tuck 1.5 = 25 x 33.5 in flat, 0.75 in margin added). The vendor's production dieline (downloaded from the product page after choosing options: "Upload Now" then "Download Dieline Template") supersedes it; every element is placed inside a panel's safe zone so it drops onto theirs without rework.
Colour mode CMYK. No spot colours (digital press). Supply hex and CMYK both; ask for a printed drawdown on kraft before the run because Ember on brown board shifts dark.
Resolution Vector throughout. If any raster is added later (photography on the lid is a v2 idea): 300 dpi at size, CMYK TIFF or PDF.
Quantities Packlane quantity picker: 1, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000 and up. Order 100 for the first batch, or 250 if the unit price step justifies it; the page's example cart shows a non-printed 250-unit run at $3.40 each ($850). Printed kraft B-flute custom 13x13x3 at 100 to 250 units: $4 to $7 each (estimate; get the instant quote on the product page, no sales call needed).
Lead time Packlane: 2D PDF proof within 24 hours of checkout, 3 business days to approve, then 7 to 15 business days production plus shipping; rush 7 business days available (https://packlane.com/products/mailer-box). Arka quotes separately (https://www.arka.com, product pages rate-limited the scraper tonight; request a quote by form).
Shipping class The filled box is about 13.5 x 13.5 x 3.5 in and 4 to 5 lb (estimate: record 180 g, hub and plate about 1.2 kg, adapter, foam). Inside USPS Priority Mail cubic tier 3 or UPS Ground zone-based. The 3 in depth keeps it under the 0.5 cu ft cubic cutoff.

2. Insert tray

Item Spec
Footprint 12.75 x 12.75 in (0.125 in clearance per side in a 13 x 13 interior).
Layers Two die-cut layers: Layer 1 top 1.25 in, Layer 2 lower 1.5 in, record in its poly-lined sleeve flat under Layer 2 on the box floor (0.2 in). Total 2.95 in in a 3.0 in interior.
Layer 1 cutouts Centre: Ø2.0 in through-pocket for the dome cap, face up, sitting exactly on the spindle axis of the record beneath. Lower left: cord and adapter pocket (ring segment). Upper right: wall plate and hardware pocket 2.9 x 1.5 in. Upper left: card slot 2.9 x 0.35 in.
Layer 2 cutouts Ø10.75 in pocket for the backplate and LED ring, with a Ø4.2 in relief in the centre for the hub.
Material First 100 units: black polyethylene foam (EPE), 1.7 pcf, die-cut, from a US foam converter (Foam Factory, UFP, or a local Uline-stocked converter; ask Packlane for their insert partner). At 500 plus: black moulded pulp tray from the same tooling drawing. Moulded pulp is the Gantri and Teenage Engineering move; foam is the right call before tooling is justified (estimate: pulp tooling $2,500 to $6,000).
Artwork brand/packaging/insert-tray.svg: plan views of both layers and a centre section with dimensions.
Why this layout The dome is the single piece of jewellery on the product, so it is the first thing seen. The record comes last, under everything, protected by two layers of foam: a record arriving warped or cracked is the one unboxing failure that cannot be fixed with an apology.

3. Insert card

Item Spec
Size 5 x 7 in, 2 sides, 0.125 in bleed.
Stock 18 pt uncoated natural white (e.g., Mohawk Superfine Eggshell or a vendor "natural uncoated" house stock); no coating, no foil.
Print Front: 3 colours (Vinyl, Halo, Ember) + Chrome Deep grey. Back: Vinyl, Ember, Chrome Deep. CMYK digital is fine.
Content Front: monogram, wordmark, "Change the record. Change the room." Back: four install steps, QR to the install page (placeholder URL spindlelighting.com/install, domain unregistered), the swap ritual, the safety paragraph.

4. Warranty card

Item Spec
Size 3.5 x 5 in, 1 side, Soot flood with Chrome and Label type.
Stock Same 18 pt uncoated as the insert card. Writable surface for serial, finish, date in pen.
Content Two-year warranty on ring, driver, hub, plate; repair or replace, shipping both ways; excludes the record, line-voltage adapters, outdoor use, opening the hub.

5. Stickers

Item Spec
Round sticker 2 in circle, Vinyl field, Chrome ring, monogram, wordmark. Matte white BOPP, kiss-cut on a roll. Goes on the box tuck as the seal and in the box as the free sticker.
Finish labels 3.0 x 0.55 in rounded rectangle, Label stock, finish dot plus "FINISH CHROME / BRASS / BLACK" and "SPINDLE 12". Applied to the dashed target on the front wall so one box SKU serves three finishes.
Files sticker-2in-round.svg, sticker-finish-labels.svg.
Vendor Sticker Mule or Packlane's packaging accessories; both take 50-unit runs.

6. Unboxing order (what the packer does)

  1. Record in poly-lined inner sleeve, flat on the box floor, label up.
  2. Layer 2 foam over it; backplate with the LED ring face down into the Ø10.75 pocket, hub nested in the relief.
  3. Layer 1 foam; wall plate, screws, anchors, felt washer and 45 adapter in the hardware pocket; adapter and cord coiled in the ring pocket; insert card and warranty card in the slot.
  4. Dome cap last, face up, in the centre pocket.
  5. Close, seal the tuck with the round sticker, apply the finish label to the front-wall target.
