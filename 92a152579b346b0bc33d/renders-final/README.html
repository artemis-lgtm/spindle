Flag, 2026-11-11 (cad/FIXES-fable52.md): two things in this image set do not match the product the CAD builds. (1) The swap shots and the kit flat-lay show a fixed "post" the record lifts off, and a loose post in the box. There is no post: the 1/4-20 stud is part of the dome cap and comes away with it. STILL OPEN. (2) CLOSED 2026-11-11 (cad/FIXES-heat-layout.md): the halo in these images was lit with an LED ring on the BACK face of the backplate aimed at the wall, and the CAD, BOM and drawings now put the strip there too (heat-safe layout: strip on an aluminium ring over a cork ring). Two small differences remain and were not re-rendered: the images put the ring 4 to 14 mm inside the backplate rim (centre R 124) and add a weaker strip on the rim; the product's strip is 14 to 22 mm inside the rim (centre R 115) and there is no rim strip. The real halo should be compared with these images on the first prototype. Do not use the swap or flat-lay shots as assembly reference.

Open index.html for the contact sheet (thumbnails link to the full PNGs).

All stills are Blender 5.0.1 Cycles (Metal GPU, never EEVEE), OpenImageDenoise, AgX view transform, 448 adaptive samples. Every PNG was opened and checked by eye after rendering. Labels are invented ("Halo Pressings", "Meridian", "Ember", "Lantern", "After Hours", "sunroom", promo blanks). No real labels, band names, logos or album art anywhere.

How it was made

  • src/make_textures.py (PIL): fictional labels, insert card, box lid print -> tex/.
  • src/final.py: every scene except the install. Geometry follows render/sconce_v2.py (1 in hub = wall gap, 10.5 in backplate, felt pads, 1/4-20 dome on a felt washer, brass insert). One change for the look: a rear-facing COB ring just inside the backplate rim (plus a weaker side strip). With the hub's 1 in gap, a rim-only strip gave a wide washed-out glow; aiming the ring at the wall gives the tight amber halo of reference 1. Worth telling engineering: the halo in these images assumes LEDs facing the wall near the rim.
  • src/install_v3.py: copy of render/sconce_v2.py (original untouched) with the camera, light, label and halo changes.
  • src/video_comp.py: the loop is built from two linear EXR passes of the same frame (room only, halo only). Light adds linearly, so ambient + k(t) x halo is exactly what a dimmer does, at the cost of two renders instead of 240. Tone-mapped with Blender's own OCIO AgX so it matches the stills.
  • Bar and studio glossy reflections use no HDR (it tinted the vinyl navy); living room exposure was raised after the first pass.
  • src/batch.sh: detached renderer (nohup, absolute PATH), skips finished files. src/build_index.py makes index.html and thumbs/.

Re-render one image: Blender -b -P src/final.py -- <scene> <wide|square|tall> --samples 448 (scenes: hero_chrome hero_brass hero_black single7 set living bar studio bedroom macro swap1 swap2 swap3 flatlay darkroom).

Open items

  • The 7 in cap is rendered as the design report's chromed 45 adapter; the 12 in dome is 1.5 in diameter, low segment of sphere. Both still need machine-shop quotes (mounting-report.md).
