# Sample run: 8 sconce bodies (6 x 12", 2 x 7") plus 5 in-wall kits

Purpose: enough finished units to photograph all three finishes, run the 2-hour thermal soak, hang two on real drywall (one in-wall, one surface), and fulfil the first of the 20 pre-sold units if Austin wants to. Nothing here is ordered; this is the shopping list with the vendor, the quantity, the price basis and the reason. Prices are from vendors.md; "live" means read off the page tonight, "est." is my estimate.

## What to order, from whom

Dome caps. PCBWay, 10 pieces, brass C360 turned to the design-report dimensions (1.5" dia low dome, soft base radius, 1/4-20 x 1" stud), polished, decorative bright chrome. Upload the STEP from render/sconce_v2.py, select Brass C360, finish "Chrome plating", and expect the plating line to be hand-corrected on the quote. Budget $120 parts plus $30 DHL (est. $12-15 each). This is the long pole at about three weeks door to door, so it goes in first. Interim so the build is not blocked: one 4-pack of Rockler solid brass knurled knobs, $7.99 live (the brass variant anyway), and a 20-pack of chrome-plated brass 1/4-20 acorn nuts for the two 7" units and the photos that only show the halo (est. $12). If Austin wants hero photos inside ten days, add 3 caps from Xometry in bright-nickel-plated brass at an estimated $30-40 each; nickel photographs the same as chrome at this size.

## Total and timeline

Parts and consumables land at roughly $1,300-1,500 with PCBWay caps, or $1,500-1,700 if the three fast Xometry nickel caps are added. The biggest single lines are the plates ($180-250), the adapters and dimmers ($160), the caps ($150), the backplates ($120-170) and the hubs ($94).

Order sequence, assuming a Monday start: day 0, PCBWay caps and Craftcloud hubs (both need the final CAD, so the CAD export from sconce_v2.py is the real first task), SendCutSend plates, Checkered records, Mouser jacks, Albany inserts, Durofelt, Uline, HitLights bundle, McMaster. Day 1, drive the DXF and a sketch to the Homestead or Hialeah CNC shop and the foam spec to Custom Foam Parts. Days 2-5, all the US stock items arrive (Uline, McMaster, Mouser, Durofelt, Albany, HitLights, records). Days 5-9, backplates and hubs. Days 7-12, plates with powder coat and foam trays. Days 15-22, chrome caps from Shenzhen. Build and thermal soak can start around day 10 with the Rockler brass knobs standing in; the chrome hero photos wait for day 22, or day 10 with the Xometry nickel option.

Checks before any order goes out: Austin's SendCutSend, PCBWay and Craftcloud accounts (I do not create accounts; he does, or he hands me the login), the final STEP and DXF exported from the Blender model with the J-slot and stud positions matching, and the brand name, because the box label and the hang sheet carry it.
