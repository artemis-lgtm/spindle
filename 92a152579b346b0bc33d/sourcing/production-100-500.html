The engineering report's $27 at 100 assumed a $1.80 brass knob, a $0.41 keyhole plate, a $3 hub and a $3.50 box. The decided mount (custom chrome dome, steel plate with PEM studs and powder, J-slot hub) adds roughly $12-15 per unit over that, and that is the honest number: at 100 units bought in the US this month the body costs about $55-57 in parts; with the cap, strip and adapter moved overseas after a sample approval it is about $40. At 500 with the same mix it is about $29-30, or about $45 all-US.

Records: Checkered Records at $2.50 each for 100 (live, $250 per 100, VG+ and genre-filtered); at 500, Record Head ($0.10 and up, $100 minimum) or Dusty Groove ($4.99 per 60, snippet) at well under $1 each plus a sorting hour per 60 to pull the ugly labels. Marquee titles remain $20-40 each per the scope report.

Margin check at the decided prices

Kit at $89: parts $56.70 (100, US) leaves $32 before shipping, processing and ads; at $40.60 (mixed) it leaves $48. Sconce at $129 with a $2.50 record: $70 and $86 respectively. Set at $249 kit / $329 with records: three bodies (two of them smaller and cheaper, call it 2.4 body-equivalents) at $136 / $97 leaves $113 / $152 on the kit set. The mixed path is what makes the Kit work at $89 under a $39 CPA; the US path makes the Kit a loss leader at 100 and only breaks even on ads at 500. Recommendation stands from the design report: the Sconce and the Set carry the margin, and the first 100 should be bought on the US path for speed and then re-sourced on approved samples.

Cash and lead time

100 units: body parts about $5,700 US path or $4,100 mixed, plus $250 records, plus about $300 for the in-wall kits if 20% take it. Lead time on the US path is governed by the caps: Xometry 7-10 business days, SendCutSend with powder 7-12 days, local CNC a week, MJF hubs 7-10 days, everything else stock. Parts in hand in about 3 weeks. On the mixed path PCBWay caps are about 3 weeks and Alibaba strip and adapters 3-5 weeks with air freight, so 5-6 weeks.

500 units: body parts about $22,400 US or $14,700 mixed, plus the pulp-tray mould (under $1,000, Pulp Packaging) and records ($500-1,500 depending on source). Alibaba caps at 500 MOQ, strip and adapters run 5-7 weeks including sea or consolidated air freight; pulp trays 6-8 weeks including the mould. Plan 8 weeks from PO to parts, and order the US-path cap and plate for the first 100 of the 500 so shipping never stops. An injection-moulded hub (aluminium tool $5,000-8,000, 2-3 weeks, Jaycon 2026 data) does not pay back until roughly 1,500-2,000 hubs against MJF at $4.50-7; skip it at 500.
