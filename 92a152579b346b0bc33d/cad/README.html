Design calls made here (not in the reports; flag if you disagree)

  1. Bayonet head = PEM FH-832-10 self-clinching stud (8-32 x 5/8) + 8-32 nylon-insert lock nut set at a 7.2 mm gauge. The mounting report said "8-32 shoulder screws"; a shoulder screw needs a tapped plate (14 ga is too thin to tap 8-32 reliably), so a pressed stud + lock nut gives the same geometry with off-the-shelf parts and an adjustable preload.
  2. Set screw sits at the 250 deg position (about 6:40) instead of exactly 6 o'clock, because the 6 o'clock position carries the surface-cord channel. The set screw lands in a hole in a bent-up tab on the plate, which locks rotation and lift in both directions; a radial screw bearing on a flat plate edge only blocks one direction.
  3. DC jack axis is offset 15 mm toward 12 o'clock so the jack body never shares the axis with the cap stud and the insert; the plate centre hole is therefore a 30 x 37 slot and the customer's wall hole is 1-1/4 in centred 6 mm above the plate centre. The jack body (10 mm long) also needs a 6.5 mm rear pocket in the backplate (hidden by the record) because the hub's 25.4 mm cannot hold pocket + web + body.
  4. Cap stud is 5/8 in, not 1 in (engineering report), so it never protrudes behind the backplate (which would hit the jack). Engagement stays at 7 to 8 threads for a 160 g record.
  5. Hub fastens to the backplate with 3x #8 x 1 in flat-head screws from the FRONT of the backplate (hidden by the record) rather than glue alone, so a hub can be swapped. Glue is still fine as a second line.
  6. The 7 in cap carries a D 37.6 x 1.6 boss that centres it in the 1.5 in hole, replacing the plastic 45 adapter for the 7 in SKU (the plastic adapter is still shipped for a 7 in on a 12 in backplate). It needs a 47 x 39 x 1.6 felt ring (custom die-cut).
  7. Thin aluminum backplates (3 / 5 mm) cannot take a rim rabbet or a 12.7 mm insert, so they are modelled as the engineering report's FLOATING configuration: 1/4-20 flat head countersunk from the back (5 mm) or pan head in the hub relief (3 mm) into the 3/8 in x 1 in F-F standoff on the front; LED is a 6 x 12 mm top-emitting COB neon-flex ring on the back face (it bends in-plane, a flat COB strip does not). Different look (double halo), own BOM line. The 1/2 in CNC MDF path is the primary.
  8. Hub recess is a uniform 5.5 deep so any pan-head wall screw or box screw clears; J-slot web 3.0 thick.

Standard dimensions used, with sources

  • LP spindle hole 0.286 in (7.26 mm); 45 rpm hole 1.5 in: engineering-report.md sec. 1 (https://allforturntables.com/2023/07/28/vinyl-record-spindle-hole/, https://www.audio-technica.com/en-us/support/audio-solutions-question-of-the-week-what-do-i-need-to-know-about-45-rpm-records).
  • E-Z Lok 400-4: 1/4-20, 0.500 in long, 25/64 in drill: https://www.albanycountyfasteners.com/1-4-20-brass-thread-inserts-wood-400-4-p/117012.htm and https://www.ezlok.com/assets/documents/EZ-LOKBrochure_2023.pdf.
  • Box spacings 83.3 mm (single gang) and 88.9 mm (round / octagon): mounting-report.md sec. 1 (verify against the specific LV bracket).
  • 14 ga steel = 0.0747 in (1.897 mm): US standard sheet gauge for steel; SendCutSend lists 0.075 in mild steel (https://sendcutsend.com/materials/processing-min-max/). Bend radius 1t and K 0.44 are typical CRS values; the vendor will apply its own K, which is why the formed STEP is the master and the DXF is marked as derived.
  • PEM FH self-clinching studs: 8-32, lengths in 1/16 in steps, clinch hole about 0.166 in for 0.060 in+ sheet (PEM bulletin FH, https://www.pemnet.com). Marked "verify" in the RFQ: the exact hole for 0.075 in sheet must be read off the bulletin.
  • 8-32 nylon-insert lock nut 11/32 in across flats (ASME B18.16.6 / common catalogue), height about 0.23 in: envelope only.
  • 1/4-20 pan head 0.492 dia x 0.133 high; 1/4 flat head 0.507 dia, 82 deg (ASME B18.6.3): envelopes for clearance only.
  • 3/8 in OD x 1 in 1/4-20 F-F aluminum standoff: https://www.aluminumspacers.com/as38-1420-64 (engineering-report sec. 1).
  • Felt washer 1 in OD x 3/8 in ID x 1/8 in, pads 1/2 in x 1/16 in: https://www.durofelt.com/categories/self-adhesive-felt-washers.
  • Panel-mount 5.5 x 2.1 DC jack with 8 mm threaded barrel (DC-022B class) and plug body about 10 mm dia: generic envelope, estimate; confirm against the jack actually bought before the hub is tooled.
  • COB strip 8 mm wide x about 1.6 thick: engineering-report sec. 2 (HitLights / Ecolocity class), estimate for thickness.
  • Halo gap 1 in, LED on the rim facing outward: engineering-report sec. 1 and 2 (sign-industry halo-letter guidance).
  • Plating spec ASTM B456 SC2 (copper + bright nickel + decorative chrome) is the normal interior decorative chrome callout; PVD and black-chrome alternatives are listed in the RFQ for the plater to choose.

Open questions

  1. Confirm the DC jack and plug actually purchased (barrel length, body size, plug body diameter) and re-run build_all.py with JACK_BARREL_L, JACK_BODY_L, POCKET_W adjusted. The hub and plate geometry depend on it.
  2. PEM FH-832 clinch hole for 0.075 in sheet: read it off bulletin FH and set STUD_HOLE_D before cutting.
  3. 7 in SKU felt ring 47 x 39 x 1.6 is not a stock Durofelt size: die-cut quote or use three 1/2 in pads on the cap underside.
  4. Aluminum variant is a different product (floating record, neon-flex ring, double halo). Keep as an upsell or drop; the decision changes which RFQ goes out.
  5. MDF jack pocket leaves 6.2 mm of MDF under the pocket; fine for MDF, check tear-out in birch ply.
  6. Hub as printed is 25.4 tall with 5.8 of material above the DC pocket; for injection moulding the designer should add drafts, core out the solid ring between R 54 and R 63 and gate at the rim. The RFQ asks the print vendor for this DFM review.
  7. Thermal: unchanged from the engineering report (strip on the rim, 0.7 in from the record edge). The first sample still needs the 2-hour IR soak before a page claim.
  8. Cosmetic threads: STEP studs and bores are plain cylinders at major / drill diameter (standard for shop quotes); the 2D sheets carry the thread callouts.
