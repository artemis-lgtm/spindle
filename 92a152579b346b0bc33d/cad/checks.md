- Centre contact: gold plunger with 4.9 mm of travel (no catalogue spring pin exceeds 3.0). With EVERY tolerance stacked the wrong way it still has 0.56 mm of compression on a 2.7 mm record and the stud tip is still 0.56 mm above the guard tube on a 1.2 mm record.
