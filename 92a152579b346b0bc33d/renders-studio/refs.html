Music room references (2026-11-10)

Lead reference: Austin's photo, Lord Decor "15 Basement Music Room Ideas With Studio Style" (tools/bridge-sessions/media/tg_7163944231_AQADyQxrG70vmEd-.jpg). What we took: charcoal fabric panels floor to ceiling, LED seams framing each wall (made warm amber so the halos own the colour), dark coffered ceiling with track and cans, drum kit centred on a round rug, walnut organ, guitar on a stand, two amps, grey carpet tiles, wood diffuser blocks on the side wall.

  • Design Milk, "The Listening Room Is Having a Design Moment": https://design-milk.com/design-forward-listening-rooms/ . Pembrooke & Ives Nashville penthouse: walnut paneling with micro-perforation. Take: warm wood against dark, the room as an instrument.
  • Panyl, 20 music studio decor ideas: https://www.panyl.com/20-music-studio-decor-interior-design-ideas/ . Take: walnut slat backdrop behind guitars on wall hangers.
  • Panyl, 20 home music room ideas: https://www.panyl.com/20-home-music-room-design-ideas/ . Take: one statement wall, everything else quiet.
  • Creatively Living, 27 record listening room ideas: https://www.creativelylivingblog.com/record-listening-room-ideas/ . Take: records displayed as art, low warm lamps.
  • Journee Mondiale, home studio room ideas: https://www.journee-mondiale.com/en/home-music-studio-room-ideas-for-serious-sound/ . Take: monitors against slats, acoustic treatment as decoration.
  • WoodyWalls acoustic panel ideas: https://woodywalls.com/blog/11-acoustic-wall-panels-ideas/ . Take: vertical slat rhythm.
  • Dezeen, Echo listening bar Rotterdam (2026-10-30): https://www.dezeen.com/2026/10/30/echo-rotterdam-listening-bar/ . Take: modular vinyl shelving across a wall, red/gold glow.
  • Dezeen, JAM Record Bar Sydney by Akin Atelier: https://www.dezeen.com/2024/08/19/akin-atelier-jam-record-bar-sydney/ . Take: Tokyo listening-bar warmth, low pockets of light.
  • Dezeen, seven listening bars lookbook: https://www.dezeen.com/2025/01/11/seven-listening-bars-lookbooks/ . Take: dim, warm, the hi-fi as the altar.
  • Hospitality Design, vinyl listening lounge trends: https://hospitalitydesign.com/projects/trends-vinyl-listening-lounges/627714 .

How designers hang a group (applied)

  • Centre of the whole group at 57-60 in, gaps 2-3 in, treat the group as one piece: https://leluxart.com/interior-design-2/gallery-wall-spacing-rules/ , https://gallery-planner.com/learn/spacing-and-alignment-tips , https://www.noonstead.com/blogs/stories/gallery-wall-layout-guide-spacing-arrangement-designer-tips
  • Grid = order and calm; single row over furniture; salon for big collections.
  • Used: (1) the Set (12 in + two 7 in) centred over the kit; (2) a row of three 12 in at 3 in gaps; (3) a 3x3 grid on the side wall as the statement wall, 3.5 in gaps.
