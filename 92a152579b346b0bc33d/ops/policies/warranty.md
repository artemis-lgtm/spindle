**What is not covered.**

- Damage from mounting hardware, wall anchors, or in-wall wiring that was installed incorrectly or against the instructions and local code, or from placing the fixture above a heat source (radiator, stove, fireplace) or in direct sun.

### Notes for Austin (not for publication)
- Two years is above the Etsy norm (most makers give none or 1 year) and cheap for us: the only parts that can fail are the COB strip, the barrel jack solder joint, and the adapter. The adapter carries its own manufacturer warranty and costs about $8.50 at qty 100 (engineering-report.md BOM), so a swap costs less than a dispute.
- Excluding the record in writing is the single most important line here. It prevents "my record is scratched" claims on a product whose whole point is a used record.
- If we ever sell a 120 V or hardwired version (mounting-report.md option C), the warranty needs a line that electrician installation is required and that we do not warrant the installation.
