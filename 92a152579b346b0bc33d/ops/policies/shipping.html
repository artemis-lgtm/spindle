Legal basis: the FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule, 16 CFR Part 435 (https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule). We must have a reasonable basis for any ship date we state; if we state none the default is 30 days from receipt of a properly completed order; if we will miss the date we must notify the buyer, offer a revised date or a refund, and silence counts as consent only for the first delay of 30 days or less. Refunds for card payments are due within one billing cycle. The pre-order section below is written to satisfy that Rule and the FTC business guide's "planned merchandise" carve-out (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule).

Shipping, in plain English

Where we ship. Every US state, plus APO/FPO addresses. We do not ship outside the US yet. (Note for Austin: the 12 V adapter ships with a US plug; international needs a different adapter and import rules, so keep it off until batch three.)

Cost. Shipping inside the contiguous US is included in the price of every sconce, kit, and set. Alaska, Hawaii, and APO/FPO addresses pay the actual carrier difference, shown at checkout before you pay.

When your order ships. - In-stock items ship within 3 business days of your order. You get a tracking number by email the moment the label prints. - Made-to-order items (named-title sconces, custom finishes) ship within 10 business days unless the product page says otherwise. - Pre-orders: see the section below.

How it ships. USPS Ground Advantage or UPS Ground, 2 to 5 business days in transit across the US. We pick the faster service for your address. Every shipment is insured for its full value.

Packaging. Each fixture ships in a double-wall box with foam corners. A record ships in its own inner sleeve, flat against the backplate, which is the stiffest thing in the box. We do not ship records loose.

Signature. Not required. If you want one, ask at checkout and we will add it free.

Address changes. Email us before the tracking number arrives and we will change it. After that the carrier controls the package and we can only request an intercept, which the carrier may refuse.

Lost or damaged. If tracking shows delivered and you do not have it, check with neighbours and your building office, then email us within 7 days; we re-ship or refund. If the box arrives damaged, photograph it before you open it fully and email us within 7 days. We re-ship first and chase the carrier ourselves.

Sales tax. Florida orders are charged Florida sales tax at checkout. Orders to other states are not charged sales tax at this time; you may owe use tax to your own state.

Pre-orders (shown only while we are taking them)

What you are buying. A place in the next batch of [BRAND] sconces, held by a $25 refundable deposit. The sconces in this batch have not been built yet. We build in batches of 100 and we order parts when the batch fills.

When it ships. The current batch is planned to ship the week of [SHIP WEEK]. That date is our honest estimate based on our parts lead times and build pace, with a two-week buffer. It is not a guarantee.

If we are late. If we cannot ship by [SHIP WEEK] we will email you before that week with a new date. You can keep your place or cancel for a full refund of everything you have paid, your choice, with one reply. If we have to move the date a second time, we will not ship without your fresh okay; if we do not hear from you, we refund you.

Cancelling. Any time before your sconce ships, for any reason, full refund of the deposit within 5 business days. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Paying the balance. About two weeks before the batch ships we email you a link for the balance (price minus your deposit). If you do not pay within 14 days of that email, we email again; if we still hear nothing after 30 days we refund your deposit and release your place.

Batch size is real. When a batch shows "100 of 100 reserved" it is full. The next deposit goes into the following batch with its own ship week, and the page says so before you pay.
