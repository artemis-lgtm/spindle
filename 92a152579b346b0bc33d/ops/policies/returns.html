Returns and Refunds

Legal basis: there is no federal or Florida law that requires a seller to accept returns of non-defective goods. Florida does require that if you do NOT give refunds you post a conspicuous notice at the point of sale; without that notice a buyer may return unused goods within 7 days (Fla. Stat. 501.142). Because we do give refunds, we post this policy anyway, linked from the product page, the cart, and the order confirmation email. The FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule (16 CFR Part 435) governs the ship-date promise and refunds for late shipment; that part is in shipping.md and repeated here in plain words. Deposits are covered at the bottom.

Returns, in plain English

30 days, no hard feelings. If you do not love it, you can return any sconce, kit, or set within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the product price. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with your order number and we will send a prepaid return label.

What we ask. - The fixture comes back in its box with all the parts: backplate and ring, hub, mounting plate and studs, dome cap and felt washer, 45 adapter, power adapter, screws and anchors, and the instruction card. Repack it the way it arrived, record (if any) in its inner sleeve against the backplate. - It has not been damaged, painted, cut, or glued to anything. - If you bought a Sconce with a record we supplied, send the record back with it. If you bought a Kit, keep your own record; it never leaves your house.

Return shipping. Inside the US we deduct a flat $12 from the refund to cover the return label, unless the item arrived damaged or defective, in which case the return is free. (See the note for Austin below on why a flat fee rather than free returns.)

Refund timing. We refund to your original payment method within 5 business days of the box arriving at our shop. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Named-title sconces. A named-title Sconce (for example one built on a specific original pressing you chose from our menu) is made to order around a record we bought for you. These can be returned in the same 30 days, but the refund is the product price less a $25 restocking fee, because we may not be able to resell that specific record at the same price.

Damaged in shipping. If the box arrives crushed or the record is cracked, do not throw anything away. Take photos of the box, the padding, and the damage, and email them to us within 7 days of delivery. We will ship a replacement at no charge and handle the carrier claim ourselves. You never have to deal with the carrier.

Defective after 30 days. That is a warranty matter; see the Limited Warranty. We fix or replace.

Exchanges. Want a different finish (chrome, brass, black) or a different record? Email us. The fastest path is usually a return plus a new order, and we will refund the return the day it arrives.

Sale items and sets. Same policy. A 3-piece set must come back complete.

What we cannot take back. Fixtures that have been installed in a wet location, modified, or damaged by a power adapter other than ours; in-wall wiring kits once the cable has been cut; records you supplied yourself.

Questions: [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We answer within two business days.

Pre-order deposits (only shown while we are taking deposits)

Deposits are fully refundable at any time before your sconce ships, no reason needed. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] and the deposit goes back to your card within 5 business days. Once your sconce ships, the deposit becomes part of the purchase and the 30-day return policy above applies to the whole order.

Notes for Austin (not for publication)

  • Flat $12 return fee: Pirate Ship USPS Ground Advantage for a 3 lb 13x13x3 box runs roughly $9 to $16 depending on zone (see ../shipping-fulfillment.md); $12 roughly breaks even and discourages "try it on" returns without being hostile. Free returns are an option if the first 100 show a return rate under 5%.
  • Expect 3 to 8% returns on a decor product bought from an ad. Budget it in the per-unit math.
  • Stripe refund: Stripe keeps the processing fee on refunds (scope-report.md section 5), so each refunded $129 order costs us about $4.04 in fees plus the two shipping legs. A returned order costs roughly $30 all-in. That is the real reason to photograph well and describe honestly.
  • Returns address: use the shop or a PO box, never a residential address if we can avoid it; it goes on every label.
