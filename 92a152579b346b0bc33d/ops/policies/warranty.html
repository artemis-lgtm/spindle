Because we use the word "Limited" and state the duration, this is a "limited written warranty" and must be available to read before purchase (a link on every product page satisfies 16 CFR 702.3 for online sales). Florida adds the implied warranty of merchantability under Fla. Stat. 672.314, which we may limit in duration but may not disclaim entirely under Magnuson-Moss once we offer any written warranty.

Who is covered. The original buyer of a [BRAND] sconce, kit, or set bought from [BRAND] directly or from a seller we authorise. The warranty does not transfer if you sell or give the product away.

What is covered. For two (2) years from the day your order is delivered, we warrant that the light fixture (the backplate, LED ring, hub, mounting plate, dome cap, and the power adapter we shipped) is free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal home use.

What we will do. If a covered part fails, we will, at our choice, repair it, replace it with the same or an equivalent part, or refund the price you paid for it. We pay return shipping for a confirmed warranty claim inside the United States. Replacement parts are covered for the remainder of the original two years.

What is not covered. - The vinyl record. Records are used, decades old, and sold as decorative material with visible wear. Wear, scratches, warps, label damage, and the condition of a record you supply yourself are never covered. - Damage from installing the fixture outdoors, in a bathroom, kitchen, or any damp or wet location. The fixture is rated for dry indoor locations only. - Use with any power adapter other than the Class 2 adapter we shipped or an identical replacement we approve. - Damage from mounting hardware, wall anchors, or in-wall wiring that was installed incorrectly or against the instructions and local code, or from placing the fixture above a heat source (radiator, stove, fireplace) or in direct sun. - Normal wear, cosmetic marks that do not affect function, modification, misuse, accident, or acts of nature. - Any product bought second-hand or from an unauthorised seller.

If the claim is covered we send a prepaid label or a replacement part, whichever solves the problem faster. Do not return anything without hearing from us first.
