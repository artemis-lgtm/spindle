Why we need one even as a tiny shop: California's CalOPPA (Bus. & Prof. Code 22575) requires any commercial site that collects personal information from California residents to post a privacy policy; the Meta pixel and Stripe both require it in their terms (Meta Business Tools Terms, https://www.facebook.com/legal/terms/businesstools; Stripe Services Agreement); and Florida's Digital Bill of Rights (Fla. Stat. 501.701 et seq.) applies only to businesses over $1 billion in revenue, so Florida itself does not bite yet. The CCPA/CPRA applies only above $25 million in revenue or 100,000 consumers, so we are outside it, but the policy below gives Californians the same rights anyway because it costs nothing. Text written to be readable, not exhaustive.

Privacy at [BRAND]

Last updated: [DATE]

We are [SELLER ENTITY], the company behind [BRAND]. This page says what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: we collect what we need to sell you a lamp and ship it, we use a couple of well-known tools to do that, and we do not sell your information.

What we collect. - When you buy or place a deposit: your name, email, shipping and billing address, phone number if you give one, what you ordered, and your preferences (era, finish, record choice). Payment card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store your full card number. - When you join the waitlist: your email and anything you type in the form. - When you contact us: whatever you send, including photos of your wall or your record. - When you visit the site: the usual technical data (IP address, browser, pages viewed, how you got here) collected by cookies and similar tools, described below.

Why we use it. - To take your order, charge you, build and ship your sconce, and answer your questions. - To send you order and shipping updates (these are not marketing and you cannot opt out of them while you have an open order). - To email you about [BRAND] news and new drops if you joined the waitlist or ticked the box. Every one of those emails has an unsubscribe link and we honour it within 10 business days (CAN-SPAM, 15 U.S.C. 7704). - To understand which ads and pages work, using the tools below. - To prevent fraud and comply with the law (tax records, for example).

Tools we use that see your data. - Stripe (payments, fraud screening): https://stripe.com/privacy - Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Pixel and Conversions API, so we can measure our ads and show them to people like you: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy - [EMAIL TOOL, e.g. Mailchimp or Resend] for the waitlist and order emails. - [HOSTING, e.g. Vercel or GitHub Pages] for the website. - USPS, UPS, and Pirate Ship for shipping labels and tracking. - Google Analytics only if we add it; we will update this page if we do. Each of these is bound by its own privacy terms and by our contract with them to use your data only to provide the service.

Cookies and your choices. The site uses a small number of cookies: one to remember your cart, and the Meta pixel's cookies for ad measurement. You can block cookies in your browser, and you can opt out of Meta's ad personalisation at https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences. The site respects the Global Privacy Control signal where our tools support it.

Who we share with. Only the service providers above, a lawyer or accountant if we need one, and the authorities if the law requires. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for other companies' advertising. If we ever sell the business, your data goes with it and the buyer takes on these promises.

Your photos. If you send us a photo of your sconce or tag us, we only use it in our marketing with your written okay (a reply saying yes counts). See ../ip-guardrails.md for the full consent flow. You can withdraw that okay any time and we take the photo down within 5 business days.

How long we keep things. Order records for 7 years (tax law). Waitlist emails until you unsubscribe. Support emails for 2 years. Website logs for 90 days.

Your rights. Wherever you live, you can email [SUPPORT EMAIL] to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it (except records the law makes us keep), or to stop marketing emails. We answer within 30 days and we never charge for it. California residents have these rights under state law, and we do not discriminate against anyone for using them.

Children. Our site is not for anyone under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If a child has given us data, email us and we delete it.

Security. Payments are handled by Stripe over encrypted connections. We keep order data in password-protected accounts with two-factor login. No system is perfect, and if there is ever a breach that affects you we will tell you as the law requires (Fla. Stat. 501.171 requires notice within 30 days).

Changes. We will post changes here with a new date. If a change matters, we email you.
