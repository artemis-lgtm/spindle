# Insurance

Written 2026-11-10. Figures marked estimate are mine, not quotes.

## What we need
Product liability (bodily injury and property damage caused by the product), $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate. A light fixture, even 12 V, is a fire and fall risk class, so this is the one policy that matters before the first unit ships. General liability usually comes bundled. Bind it the day the first deposit is taken, not the day the first box ships (scope-report.md).

## Questions to ask every carrier
1. Is a hand-assembled 12 V LED wall light accepted, or excluded as electrical?
2. Does the policy cover the in-wall installation the customer performs?
3. Products-completed operations included?
4. Vendor endorsement available (marketplaces ask for it)?
5. Who is the named insured: the entity Austin chooses in ops/entity.md, not HI and not Elbo.
